Cybersecurity Act 2018 (Singapore)

An Act to provide for the oversight and maintenance of national cybersecurity in
Singapore, and to establish a framework for the protection of critical information
infrastructure against cybersecurity threats.

Section 7. Designation of critical information infrastructure. The Commissioner may, by
written notice, designate a computer or computer system as a critical information
infrastructure for the purposes of this Act if the Commissioner is satisfied that the
computer or computer system is necessary for the continuous delivery of an essential
service, and the loss or compromise of the computer or computer system will have a
debilitating effect on the availability of the essential service in Singapore.

Section 11. Codes of practice and standards of performance. The Commissioner may issue
or approve one or more codes of practice or standards of performance for the regulation
of the owners of critical information infrastructure with respect to measures to be
taken by them to ensure the cybersecurity of the critical information infrastructure.

Section 14. Duty to report cybersecurity incidents. The owner of a critical information
infrastructure must notify the Commissioner of the occurrence of any prescribed
cybersecurity incident in respect of the critical information infrastructure within the
prescribed period after becoming aware of such occurrence.

Section 19. National cybersecurity. The Commissioner is responsible for the oversight
and promotion of cybersecurity in Singapore at the national level, including the
establishment of cybersecurity codes of practice and national response measures.
