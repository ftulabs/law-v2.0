MONETARY AUTHORITY OF SINGAPORE

NOTICE 655   NOTICE ON CYBER HYGIENE

This Notice is issued pursuant to the Banking Act and applies to all banks in Singapore.
It sets out the cyber hygiene measures that a bank, as a licensed financial institution,
must put in place to protect its information technology systems.

Paragraph 4. Administrative accounts. A bank must ensure that every administrative
account in respect of any operating system, database, application, security appliance
or network device is secured to prevent unauthorised access.

Paragraph 5. Security patches. A bank must apply security patches to address security
vulnerabilities in every system in a timely manner.

Paragraph 6. Security standards. A bank must establish and implement written security
standards for every system, and must ensure that each system conforms to the security
standards.

This Notice applies only to the financial sector and does not constitute a national
cybersecurity framework of general application.
